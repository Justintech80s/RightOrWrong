# Written narrative rules for this edition

All conversations are written on screen. Speaker names, narrative descriptions and player choices carry the story. No voiced dialogue, voice-actor casting or speech service is required for this edition. Sound effects and music may be added separately later.

The current draft uses General **Adrian Ward**, Coach **Imani Brooks**, recruit **Jordan Cross**, Captain **Mara Keene** and fixer **Nico Rellan**, following the later cast document in the recovered source. A civilian reviewer handles the independent oversight follow-up. Earlier design files contain different placeholder names; this document and the runtime dialogue identify the current draft consistently.

The recruit chooses the first sport freely. Its relationship effect stays behind the scenes: Judo, Archery and Sport Shooting seed a law-enforcement introduction; Karate, Taekwondo and Fencing seed a criminal introduction. The selected sport is not itself treated as a moral decision.

Placement tiers use the first successful assessment score, rather than accumulated practice XP: 60–64 freelance/underfunded, 65–79 neighborhood, 80–94 established, 95–100 elite. The criminal introduction distinguishes knowledgeable but disorganized freelancers from disciplined, powerful organizations. These are tunable demo thresholds, not official sports grading standards.

Jordan's dilemma remains part of Mission 1 regardless of sport. The player can report a score discrepancy and request a fair retest, or protect Jordan by keeping it private. Later dialogue acknowledges the choice.

The first contact can be accepted or declined. Declining offers an academy assignment. All players can then choose command, civilian oversight or criminal contacts for the records, and reconsider their direction at the follow-up. Future chapters can deepen these relationships without locking the opening into a permanent good/evil class.

The evaluation ends the playable-demo storyline. It is not a claim that the full academy curriculum, single-player campaign or online world is complete.
