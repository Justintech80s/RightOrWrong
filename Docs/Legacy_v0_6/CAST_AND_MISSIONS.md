# Character and mission writing bible — original satire

These are **new fictional characters** evocative of broad movie/TV archetypes, not replicas of protected characters or their dialogue.

| Figure | Public role | Behind the scenes | Pedagogy / gameplay role |
|---|---|---|---|
| General Adrian Ward | Decorated Vanguard leader | Defends institution while suspecting donor interference | Discipline, leadership, contested orders |
| Elias Vale | Investor and philanthropist | Funds policing and redevelopment with conflicting incentives | Institutional incentives, PR missions |
| Imani Brooks | Community sports coach | Knows how the city excludes talented athletes | Mentoring and civic judgment |
| Jordan Cross | Vanguard recruit, rival | Craves approval more than truth | Rivalry, friendship and loyalty |
| Captain Mara Keene | Oversight investigator | Has limited resources and political pressure | Evidence, ethics, case work |
| Nico Rellan | Dockside fixer | Sells secrets to both factions | Crime introductions, conflicting stories |
| Mayor Celia Voss | Campaign-first executive | Loves press conferences more than specifics | Political satire and public trust |

## Mission outlines

**ROW_004 — The Unmarked Syllabus:** A civics lesson becomes the player's first real dialogue choice. Objective: inspect two conflicting accounts of a donor report. Consequence: Knowledge XP, altered public trust/investor favor. Implemented as text interaction in v0.4.

**ROW_005 — The Mentor Who Argues Back:** Talk to Imani Brooks about public service. Ask for feedback or challenge her conclusions; both are productive ways to learn. Consequence: mentorship completion and knowledge XP. Implemented as text interaction in v0.4.

**ROW_006 — Commencement, With Conditions:** Complete minimum primary sport training, a seminar, a mentor conversation, and the first case aftermath. Consequence: graduated flag and law reputation. Implemented as a labeled marker, not a cinematic graduation.

**DEMO_SETPIECE_A — Donor Day:** Public-relations event turns into a pursuit of a missing case file. Satire: billionaire pitches transparency while hiring people to hide embarrassing paperwork. Planned only.

**DEMO_SETPIECE_B — A Training Accident:** Rival tries to cover a mistake; player's response affects academy relationships and a later investigation. Planned only.

**DEMO_SETPIECE_C — The Wrong Convoy:** Police, contract security, and civic activists converge on the same event; playable options produce different witnesses and evidence. Planned only.
